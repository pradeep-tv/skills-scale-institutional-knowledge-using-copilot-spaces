# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work directly with **Technical Leads** on architecture and design decisions
- Collaborate with **QA/Testing Lead** on acceptance criteria and test coverage
- Coordinate with **Product Managers** on feature priorities and requirements
- Report technical risks to **Project Managers** and **Technical Leads**

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work with **Product Lead** on strategic alignment and decision approval
- Collaborate with **Developers** and **Technical Leads** on feasibility and trade-offs
- Coordinate with **QA/Testing Lead** on acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Report to **Project Sponsors** on outcomes and impact metrics

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Escalate blockers and risks to **Product Lead** and **Project Sponsor**
- Coordinate with **Delivery Coordinator** on logistics and communication
- Work with all roles to track progress and manage dependencies
- Report project status to **Product Lead** and stakeholders

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA and Testing professionals ensure product quality, validate acceptance criteria, and manage test strategy. They collaborate with developers and product managers to define testability requirements and maintain quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and execute test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Conduct manual and automated testing for feature acceptance
- Identify, triage, and track quality issues
- Participate in Definition of Done and test coverage planning
- Validate release readiness and coordinate smoke testing
- Provide quality metrics and trend analysis

### Goals
- Ensure features meet acceptance criteria before release
- Minimize critical defects reaching production
- Maintain high test coverage and automation standards
- Reduce time spent on regression testing

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and acceptance criteria review
- QA status updates in daily standups
- Test execution reports and quality metrics
- Bug triage and root cause discussion
- Pre-release sign-off communications

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with **Developers** on test coverage and acceptance criteria
- Align with **Product Managers** on feature specifications and acceptance standards
- Coordinate with **Technical Leads** on testing strategy and infrastructure
- Report quality metrics to **Project Managers** and **Product Lead**
- Participate in pre-release validation with **Developers** and **Project Managers**

---

## Technical Lead / Engineering Manager

### Role Summary
Technical Leads own the technical architecture, design decisions, and engineering practices for the project. They work closely with the Product Manager and developers to balance feature delivery with technical sustainability.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve technical designs and architecture decisions
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Mentor developers and conduct code reviews
- Manage engineering team capacity and skill development
- Champion testing, observability, and code quality standards
- Participate in planning and estimation

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and production incidents
- Improve team velocity and code quality over time
- Enable knowledge sharing and skill growth

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Engineering-focused risk assessment
- Code review comments and mentoring
- Capacity and resource planning discussions
- Technical debt tracking and prioritization

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Guide **Developers** on architectural decisions and best practices
- Collaborate with **Product Managers** on feasibility and trade-offs
- Work with **QA/Testing Lead** on testability and quality standards
- Report technical risks to **Project Managers** and **Product Lead**
- Participate in sprint planning with **Project Managers**

---

## Product Lead

### Role Summary
Product Leads provide strategic oversight, approval authority, and escalation point for product decisions. They work at the intersection of business strategy, customer needs, and technical feasibility.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and key decisions
- Align projects with broader product strategy and roadmap
- Resolve conflicts between competing priorities
- Act as escalation point for cross-team dependencies and blockers
- Review metrics and success outcomes post-release
- Sponsor process improvements and organizational learning

### Goals
- Ensure projects align with business and product strategy
- Maximize customer impact and ROI
- Reduce decision-making delays through clear authority and frameworks
- Foster cross-team alignment and collaboration

### Typical Communication
- Weekly PM + PdM + Product Lead alignment
- Project charter and milestone approvals
- Stakeholder escalation and decision-making
- Monthly strategic review and retrospective review
- Post-release outcome analysis

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Approve decisions for **Product Managers** and provide strategic direction
- Escalate and resolve blockers from **Project Managers**
- Review technical risks and decisions from **Technical Leads**
- Provide guidance and approval to **Project Sponsors**
- Participate in post-release reviews with the entire delivery team

---

## Project Sponsor

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors are business owners who provide funding, set business priorities, and provide high-level oversight. They ensure projects deliver business value and have visibility into major risks or changes.

### Responsibilities
- Fund and approve project initiation and key milestones
- Define and approve success metrics and business outcomes
- Provide business context and priority guidance
- Review status reports and key decisions
- Support escalation and removal of organizational blockers
- Participate in retrospectives to capture lessons learned

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain alignment between project delivery and business strategy
- Minimize project delays and unplanned costs
- Foster organizational learning and process improvement

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or milestone-based status reports
- Project approval and decision gates
- Escalation of high-impact blockers or risks
- Release announcements and business metrics review
- Retrospective attendance and sponsorship of improvements

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Receive escalated decisions from **Product Lead**
- Provide direction to **Product Managers** on business priorities
- Review project status from **Project Managers**
- Participate in retrospectives with the delivery team
- Approve high-level resource and timeline decisions

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance professionals ensure projects meet security and regulatory requirements. They work with the team to integrate security scanning, threat assessment, and incident response protocols.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements and standards for projects
- Review and approve security designs for new features
- Configure and monitor security scanning in CI/CD
- Triage security findings and coordinate remediation
- Lead security incident response and post-incident reviews
- Maintain compliance with applicable regulations
- Provide security training and guidance to the team

### Goals
- Deliver secure features that protect customer and company data
- Reduce security vulnerabilities and incidents
- Maintain compliance with security and regulatory requirements
- Enable the team to build security into their workflow

### Typical Communication
- Security requirements in project planning
- Security design reviews and threat assessments
- Security scan results and remediation tracking
- Security incident communication and response
- Security metrics and trend reports

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with **Developers** and **Technical Leads** on security design and implementation
- Define security acceptance criteria with **Product Managers** and **QA/Testing Lead**
- Report security risks and compliance issues to **Project Managers** and **Product Lead**
- Collaborate with **Project Managers** on incident response and remediation
- Coordinate with **Project Sponsor** on high-impact security decisions

---

## Delivery Coordinator

### Role Summary
Delivery Coordinators manage project logistics, communications, and administrative tasks. This role is particularly valuable for distributed or larger teams where coordination overhead is significant.

### Responsibilities
- Schedule and facilitate project meetings (kickoff, planning, standups, retros)
- Maintain project documentation and decision logs
- Track and communicate project status and metrics
- Coordinate dependencies and cross-team communications
- Manage project board and workflow
- Support PM and PdM with administrative and organizational tasks

### Goals
- Reduce coordination overhead and meeting load on core team
- Maintain transparency and clear communication across stakeholders
- Enable PM/PdM to focus on strategy and decision-making
- Improve information accessibility and consistency

### Typical Communication
- Meeting invitations and agendas
- Status reports and project updates
- Communication with stakeholders and dependent teams
- Project board updates and documentation maintenance
- Action item tracking and follow-up

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Support **Project Managers** with meeting facilitation and documentation
- Coordinate with all team members on communication and status tracking
- Assist **Product Managers** with agenda preparation and stakeholder updates
- Help track decisions and action items from all team members
- Support **Product Lead** with escalation tracking and meeting coordination
